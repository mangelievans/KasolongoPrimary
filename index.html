A message from school leadership

Foreword from the school heads

The Head Teacher

Education contributes to a balanced and thriving society. Without education, the world would not be able to develop, connect, and benefit from the inventions that shape our lives today.

The role of teachers in every child's life can never be overlooked. God has blessed this school with hardworking teachers who, together with me, continue to move the school forward.

We have seen a positive change in our mean score in recent years, reaching 350 marks in 2025. I thank every teacher who remained committed and helped us achieve that milestone.

Our disciplined students have also contributed greatly to this achievement. I thank their parents for their continued cooperation and support.

May God bless you.

The Deputy Head Teacher

I smile when I remember how far we have come. Since the school was established, it has never experienced progress as significant as the change we are seeing today.

We have worked with the entire Kasolongo community to provide the best possible education for our children.

May God bless us all.

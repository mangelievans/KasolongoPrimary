About Us

Kasolongo primary school was founded in 1960.


It was the second school to be founded after Mamiloki primary(1955).
The school is located 5Kms from Kithangathini off Machakos-Kitui road
The school is founded under the Catholic faith.
The school has produced very many charismatic leaders including:
  1. Dr. Raymond Musyoka-University of Nairobi
  2. Evans Mangeli-Meru University
  3. The late. Johnson Nzioka-Former KEPSHA Chairperson

The school has seen a greater improvement in recent years with a new field, courtesy of Hillary Mutyambai.

We celebrate our former students!

The school celebrates a group of successful people who were once students here.

  1. Hillary Mutyambai

    Hillary is the former Inspector General of Police. He was a hardworking and disciplined pupil, and his early promise pointed towards a successful future.

  2. Dr. Raymond Musyoka

    Dr. Raymond is a lecturer at the University of Nairobi. From a hardworking young boy, he became a senior lecturer at one of Kenya's leading institutions.

    Raymond has contributed significantly to community development and is an active member of the Catholic Church.

  3. Johnson Nzioka

    Johnson Nzioka, who is late, was another important student of the school. He was a bright pupil who pursued further education and was eventually named head of the primary school teachers' association.

    Before his death, he was also the head teacher at Dohnholm Primary School in Nairobi.

  4. Evans Mangeli

    Having completed his primary education in 2018, Evans was a valued student of the school. A sharp and promising young person, he represents the younger generation of students the school has produced.

    He is currently a student at Meru University of Science and Technology.
